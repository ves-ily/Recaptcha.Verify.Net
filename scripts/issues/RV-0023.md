RV-0023: Quality gates (dotnet format, TreatWarningsAsErrors)

**Problem**
No style/analyzer enforcement and the library does not treat warnings as errors; style drift accumulates.

**Location**
csproj (no `<TreatWarningsAsErrors>`, no `<AnalysisLevel>`); no `dotnet format` CI step.

**Fix**
- Add `dotnet format --verify-no-changes` to CI; enable `<TreatWarningsAsErrors>` + analysis level for the library.

**Priority:** P5 (infra)
